The idea behind this blog

Everyday at PPSU is a running journal of the week-to-week reality of studying at P P Savani University — less about big events, more about what a normal Tuesday actually involves.

Why keep a blog like this

Prospective students usually get two versions of university life: the polished brochure version, and the venting-on-social-media version. Neither one covers the actual in-between — waiting for a lab system to free up, splitting a plate of Maggi between four people, or finishing an assignment at midnight because the day got away from everyone.

This site began as an assignment for a web development course — built with plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, no frameworks. It kept going afterward because a week-by-week record of campus life turned out to be genuinely worth keeping.

A quick note on P P Savani University

P P Savani University sits in Kosamba, in the Surat district of Gujarat, and runs programs spanning engineering, management, pharmacy, design, and the sciences. Everything on this site is one student's personal account, not an official university publication.
